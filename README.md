# THE NEXT PREDICTION · NTP

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **NIPHĒLEKTRON**.

THE NEXT PREDICTION (NTP) — the domain's engine: strip away everything an AI seems to do and one act remains — predict the NEXT TOKEN. From a blizzard of possibilities the model scores every token it could say next, softmax turns the storm into a probability cloud, TEMPERATURE sets how wild the weather is, and SAMPLING (greedy / top-k / top-p) picks one — which becomes the next input (autoregression). The electron choosing its path through the snowstorm, one step at a time. ⚠ STRICTLY two-layer: the mechanism is settled, but whether next-token prediction merely mimics understanding (the 'stochastic parrot') or whether predicting well enough requires a real world-model is the deepest OPEN argument in AI — presented as the live question, not settled.
